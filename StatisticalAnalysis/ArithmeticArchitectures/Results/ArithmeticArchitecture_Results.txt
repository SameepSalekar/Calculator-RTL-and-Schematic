5. OVERALL OBSERVATIONS
-----------------------

1. Fixed-Point uses fewer implementation LUTs,
   slices, and registers than Integer.

2. FP16 requires more FPGA resources than the
   narrower Integer and Fixed-Point architectures.

3. FP16 has a substantially longer reported timing
   path, but timing constraints differ.

4. FP16 meets the specified 40 ns clock constraint.

5. Power results require consistent switching activity,
   clock settings, and implementation conditions
   before meaningful efficiency rankings can be made.

6. A controlled comparison should use matching FPGA
   devices, timing constraints, and workloads.
